About and privacy

What “true size” means

The 3D model is built from the measurements the gallery entered, so its size is exact. How exactly it then sits on your wall depends on your phone’s camera tracking, the light and the wall itself; plain, evenly lit walls are the hardest. Use the calibration target and a tape measure to see the accuracy on your own phone.

Colour

A phone photograph shown on an uncalibrated screen cannot reproduce paint exactly. Treat colour as a guide and ask the gallery for professional photographs.

Privacy

Viewing in AR happens on your phone; no camera images or room data are sent anywhere. Public pages count page views and button taps without cookies, accounts or location. The studio keeps photos on your device unless you sign in to a gallery account and upload them.
