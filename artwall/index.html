A painting on your wall, at its true size

Gallery staff photograph a painting, confirm its corners and measurements, and publish it. A buyer opens a link and sees the work hanging on their own wall in augmented reality, at the size it really is. No app, no account.

Try it with the test chartPhotograph a painting

On iPhone, use Safari. On Android, use Chrome on an ARCore phone. On a computer, open a work and scan its code with your phone.

1 · Photograph

Point the phone at the work. ArtWall suggests the corners; you can move every one of them.

2 · Measure

Enter the real width, height and depth. Size always comes from your measurements, never from the photo.

3 · Hang

The buyer taps “View on my wall”. The work appears upright on the wall at its measured size, which cannot be pinched bigger.

This is the phone build: the demo, the AR test and the studio run entirely on your device. Gallery accounts and publishing need the hosted version.
